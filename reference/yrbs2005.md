# Youth Risk Behavior Survey, 2005 (national public-use file)

Twelve dichotomous health-risk-behavior items for U.S. high school
students, reproducing the empirical illustration used throughout Collins
& Lanza (2010), *Latent Class and Latent Transition Analysis* (sections
1.8.1.2, 2.4, 4.6.4 and 5.12.2). The items are CDC's own precomputed
dichotomous recodes (the `QN*` variables in the CDC file), so they match
CDC's and Collins & Lanza's coding exactly. `grade` is included to
reproduce the multiple-group analysis in sec. 5.12.2; `weight`, `psu`
and `stratum` reproduce the complex sample design and can be passed to
[`fit_mixture()`](https://pdvalencia.github.io/mixtureEM/reference/fit_mixture.md)'s
`weights=`, `cluster=` and `strata=` arguments.

## Usage

``` r
yrbs2005
```

## Format

A data frame with 13,917 rows (respondents) and 17 columns:

- grade:

  Factor, grade in school: `"9"`, `"10"`, `"11"`, `"12"`.

- sex:

  Factor: `"Female"`, `"Male"`.

- smoked_before_13:

  Smoked a whole cigarette before age 13 (0/1).

- smoked_daily_30d:

  Smoked cigarettes daily for 30 days (0/1).

- drove_drinking:

  Drove after drinking alcohol, past 30 days (0/1).

- first_drink_before_13:

  Had a first drink of alcohol before age 13 (0/1).

- binge_drink_30d:

  Had 5 or more drinks in a row, past 30 days (0/1).

- marijuana_before_13:

  Tried marijuana before age 13 (0/1).

- cocaine_ever:

  Used cocaine, ever (0/1).

- glue_ever:

  Sniffed glue or inhalants, ever (0/1).

- meth_ever:

  Used methamphetamines, ever (0/1).

- ecstasy_ever:

  Used ecstasy, ever (0/1).

- sex_before_13:

  Had sexual intercourse before age 13 (0/1).

- sex_4plus_partners:

  Had sexual intercourse with four or more people, ever (0/1).

- weight:

  Analysis (sampling) weight.

- psu:

  Primary sampling unit identifier.

- stratum:

  Sampling stratum identifier.
